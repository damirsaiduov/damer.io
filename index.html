is it ai?

no, it's Actual Intelligence. my friends call me Damer.

at around 14, i started my first business. the idea was simple: help people who needed it. it went on to bring in over $100k in gross revenue, and i directed the proceeds toward supporting more than 200 low-income families across kazakhstan. making money wasn't really the point.

later, i started my own academy to make physics and the fundamentals of engineering a little less intimidating. built the website, launched online courses, had 250 people register, and ran 10 offline workshops. turns out teaching is a great way to realize how much you still have to learn.

somewhere along the way, i also built a controlled security-testing environment for ai coding agents. simulated github repositories, documents, and webpages with instructions the agents weren't supposed to follow. mostly to see whether they'd trust the user or a random file on the internet. purely for research, obviously :).

i've also worked on Green-Basket, a green-tech startup trying to make sustainable food habits less of a chore.

these days i'm really into robotics and machine learning. i was a research assistant at NU TACTILE LAB, and i'm currently on the Astrobot FTC team. getting things to work in the real world is a different kind of debugging.

i was once a kid riding a quad bike alone in the mountains near almaty and nearly ended up underneath it. that's a different story.
