is it ai?

no, it's actual intelligence. my name is damir.

at around 14, i started my first business. the idea was pretty simple: help people who needed it. it eventually brought in over $100k in gross revenue, with the proceeds going toward supporting more than 200 low-income families across kazakhstan. for once, making money wasn't really the point.

later, i started my own academy to help young people understand physics and the fundamentals of engineering. built the website, launched online courses, got 250 people to register, and ran 10 offline workshops. explaining how things work turned out to be a pretty good way to learn, too.

somewhere along the way, i also built a controlled security-testing environment for ai coding agents: simulated github repositories, docs, and webpages with instructions the agents definitely weren't supposed to follow. mostly to see whether they'd listen to the user or to a random file on the internet. strictly for research, obviously :).

i've also worked on green-basket, a little attempt to make sustainable food habits less of a chore.

right now i'm really into robotics and machine learning. i was a research assistant at nu tactile lab, and i'm currently part of the astrobot team in first tech challenge. i like things that are difficult to make work in the real world.

i was also once an 8-year-old riding a quad bike alone in the mountains near almaty and nearly ended up underneath it. that's a story for another page.
